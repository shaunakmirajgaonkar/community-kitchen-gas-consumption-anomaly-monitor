# Dashboard Guide

Overview: high-level KPIs and distributions.

Anomaly Matrix: component-by-kitchen comparison.

Consumption & Meals: usage efficiency and meal-volume relationships.

Equipment Condition: stove, regulator, hose, and storage condition.

Supply & Deliveries: delivery gaps and LPG balance.

Kitchen Profiles: community-level comparison.

Priority Queue: ordered review table.

Scenario Lab: before-vs-after what-if analysis.

Reports & Export: CSV and Markdown exports.

Data Explorer: filtered record inspection.
