# Responsible Use

This application is a screening and planning aid. It does not confirm gas leakage, equipment failure, legal non-compliance, or a hazardous condition. Scores are based only on the locally supplied fields and transparent heuristics in `analytics.py`.

Never rely on the dashboard alone for an emergency. A suspected gas leak, damaged hose, regulator fault, cylinder issue, or other immediate safety concern requires the applicable emergency response and inspection procedures for the location.

The sample dataset is synthetic demonstration data.
